PROJECT

About Aqua Sentinel

AI-powered water intelligence prototype

Aqua Sentinel is a student project concept for reservoir information, water-quality analysis, forecasting support and public awareness. Actual monitoring requires verified data sources, sensors and a connected backend.

Potential reference organizations / portals

  • Central Water Commission (CWC)
  • Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB)
  • India Meteorological Department (IMD)
  • National Water Data Portal
  • Tamil Nadu water-resource departments

Listing an organization does not mean this prototype is connected to or endorsed by it.
